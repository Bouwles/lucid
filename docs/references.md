# References

The research LUCID builds on.

- Valevski, Leviathan, Arar and Fruchter. *Diffusion Models Are Real-Time Game Engines* (GameNGen), 2024. [arXiv:2408.14837](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14837). The context-noise trick against drift comes from here.
- Alonso, Jelley, Micheli, Kanervisto, Storkey, Pearce and Fleuret. *Diffusion for World Modeling: Visual Details Matter in Atari* (DIAMOND), 2024. [arXiv:2405.12399](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12399). EDM-based world models, few sampling steps, and the two-stage dynamics plus upsampler design.
- Karras, Aittala, Aila and Laine. *Elucidating the Design Space of Diffusion-Based Generative Models* (EDM), 2022. [arXiv:2206.00364](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00364). Preconditioning, noise distribution, loss weighting and samplers.
- Ha and Schmidhuber. *World Models*, 2018. [arXiv:1803.10122](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10122).
- Bruce et al. *Genie: Generative Interactive Environments*, 2024. [arXiv:2402.15391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15391).
- *Toward Stable World Models: Measuring and Addressing World Instability in Generative Environments*, 2025. [arXiv:2503.08122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08122). Refinement sampling and stability measurement.
- Ho and Salimans. *Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance*, 2022. [arXiv:2207.12598](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12598).
- Meng et al. *SDEdit: Guided Image Synthesis and Editing with Stochastic Differential Equations*, 2021. [arXiv:2108.01073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.01073). The idea behind refinement sampling.
